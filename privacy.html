Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Movie Club is a free site for running a movie club with friends. This page explains what it stores and who can see it.

What we store

  • Your Google sign-in: your email address and the name and picture on your Google account. We use your email only to sign you in.
  • What you add to your clubs: your name in each club, screenings, ratings, reviews, votes, missed screenings and party-mode images.
  • Your watchlist: films you saved to pick later.
  • Display settings such as color palettes are saved in your browser only, not on our servers.

Who can see it

  • Everything in a club is visible only to that club's members. Nobody can see a club without joining it, and joining needs the club's invite link.
  • Your watchlist is visible only to you.
  • Your email address isn't shown to other members.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in and image storage.
  • Google handles sign-in.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) supplies film search, details and posters. Your browser contacts TMDB directly when you search for a film. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • GitHub Pages serves the site.

We don't sell your data, show ads or use tracking or analytics.

Leaving and deleting

  • Leave a club from its settings (the gear icon). Your name and ratings stay in the club's history, no longer linked to your account.
  • Delete your account from the gear icon in a club, or at the bottom of your clubs page. This removes your sign-in and your watchlist, and unlinks you from every club. A club nobody else has joined is deleted along with everything in it.
  • A club admin can delete their club, which removes everything in it for everyone.
